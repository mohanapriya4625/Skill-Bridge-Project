Percentages

Learn the basics and master percentage calculations.

What is Percentage?

Percentage means "per hundred". It is used to represent a number as a fraction of 100.

💡 Simple Concept

If you score 80 marks out of 100, your percentage is 80%. If you score 40 out of 50, convert it into a percentage using the formula below.

Basic Formula

Percentage Formula
Percentage = (Obtained Value ÷ Total Value) × 100
Solved Example
Example: 72 is what percentage of 240?
Percentage = (72 ÷ 240) × 100

= 0.3 × 100

= 30%
Answer: 30%
Quick Example
Find 20% of ₹1000.
20% of 1000
= (20 ÷ 100) × 1000
= 200
Answer: ₹200
